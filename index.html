What can this assistant help with?

It can support work across the web, files and code, productivity apps, documents, media, and task automation. Ask for a single task or combine several capabilities to produce a larger deliverable.

The sections below reflect the capability list you provided. Access to a specific service depends on its connection, permissions, and current configuration.

2

3

4

5
DELIVERShare the result for your review. Recurring work can be scheduled or run in the background.
